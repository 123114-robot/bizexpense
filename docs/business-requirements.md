# Business Requirements

- Reduce manual re-keying while retaining human control over extracted values.
- Provide a single searchable expense register for a demo SME.
- Surface total spend, current-month spend, GST paid and record count from stored data.
- Preserve invoices alongside expense records.
- Keep the foundation explainable, testable and ready for later Australian accounting workflows.

Out of scope: authentication, multi-tenancy, tax advice, bank feeds, approvals and accounting integrations.
